Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you buy and use WoW Savant. WoW Savant is sold by WoW Savant, an Australian sole trader. The short version: we collect as little as possible and we never sell it.

Stored on your PC

The app stores your settings, licence key and chat history locally on your own PC. We don't receive a copy.

Sent to your Grok Bot account

To answer you, your questions and any screenshots you paste are sent to your own Grok Bot account. xAI's privacy terms apply to that data.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe handles your card details under its own privacy policy – we never see or store your full card number. Stripe keeps a record of your purchase (such as your email address, billing country and payment history) so we can provide receipts, refunds and support.

Licence checks

To check your licence, the app sends your licence key and a random ID for your PC to our licence server. The licence server stores only your Stripe customer ID, the ID of the PC your licence is activated on, and your licence status. It does not store your card details, chat history or screenshots.

Announcements

The app checks a public announcements file on this website for news about WoW Savant.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use ads or trackers, in the app or on this website.

Support emails

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email wowsavant.support@gmail.com.
