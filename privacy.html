Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use WoW Savant. Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller. The short version: we collect as little as possible and we never sell it.

Stored on your PC

The app stores your settings and chat history locally on your own PC. We don't receive a copy.

Sent to your Grok Bot account

To answer you, your questions and any screenshots you paste are sent to your own Grok Bot account. xAI's privacy terms apply to that data.

Licence checks

To check your licence, the app sends your keys and a PC instance id to Lemon Squeezy.

Purchases

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and handles your purchase details, including payment, tax and receipts, under its own privacy policy.

Announcements

The app checks a public announcements file on this website for news about WoW Savant.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use ads or trackers, in the app or on this website.

Support emails

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email wowsavant.support@gmail.com.
