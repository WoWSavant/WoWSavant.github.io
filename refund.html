Refund policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

We want you to be happy with WoW Savant. Here's how refunds work, in plain English. WoW Savant is sold by WoW Savant, an Australian sole trader, and payments are processed by Stripe.

When you can get a refund

If WoW Savant doesn't work on a supported system, email us at wowsavant.support@gmail.com and we'll try to fix it. If we can't get it working, you can have a refund as long as you ask within 14 days of purchase.

A supported system is 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 on an Intel or AMD (x86-64) PC, running the game in windowed or borderless mode, with your own Grok subscription and Grok Bot connected to the PC.

When we can't offer a refund

  • Unsupported systems. Windows 7 or 8, Windows on ARM, Mac, Linux and exclusive fullscreen mode are listed as not supported on the website before you buy.

We can't offer a refund if WoW Savant loses some or all of its function because of changes or actions by the game publisher, such as game updates, interface or add-on changes, or changes to their rules or policies. This is outside our control. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law still apply.

Licence and subscription

WoW Savant costs a one-off $20 USD licence, which is yours forever, plus a $3 USD/month subscription. You pay both together in one checkout. You can cancel your subscription any time; WoW Savant keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for, then stops until you restart it. Your licence is never charged twice – if you come back later, you only restart the $3/month. Payments are processed by Stripe. Prices are in US dollars, and your bank may add currency conversion fees. Your Grok subscription is separate and is paid to xAI. Once you cancel your subscription, you won't be charged again.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. It can take 5–10 business days for your bank to show it.
