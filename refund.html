Refund policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

We want you to be happy with WoW Savant. Here's how refunds work, in plain English.

When you can get a refund

If WoW Savant doesn't work on a supported system, email us at wowsavant.support@gmail.com and we'll try to fix it. If we can't get it working, you can have a refund as long as you ask within 14 days of purchase.

A supported system is 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 on an Intel or AMD (x86-64) PC, running WoW in windowed or borderless mode, with your own Grok subscription and Grok Bot connected to the PC.

When we can't offer a refund

  • Unsupported systems. Windows 7 or 8, Windows on ARM, Mac, Linux and WoW in exclusive fullscreen are listed as not supported on the store page before you buy.
  • No Grok subscription. WoW Savant needs your own Grok subscription with Grok Bot. This is stated clearly before you buy and is not included in the price.

Licence and subscription

WoW Savant is sold in two parts, and you need both: a one-off $30 USD Licence, which is yours forever, and a $2 USD/month subscription (WoW Savant Access). You can cancel your subscription anytime. WoW Savant keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for, then stops until you restart your subscription. Your Licence is never charged twice – if you come back later, you only restart the $2/month. Once you cancel your subscription, you won't be charged again.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record.

Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law or other consumer laws that apply to you.
